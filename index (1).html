Yevgeniia's
Asian Odyssey

Thailand  ·  Indonesia  ·  Malaysia  ·  Vietnam

In the spring of 2024, Yevgeniia Goncharenko embarked on a four-country backpacking adventure across Southeast Asia — a region that blends ancient temples, vibrant street markets, emerald rice terraces, and some of the world's most breathtaking coastlines. From the bustling night bazaars of Bangkok to the volcanic peaks of Bali, every stop brought new flavors, faces, and unforgettable memories.
